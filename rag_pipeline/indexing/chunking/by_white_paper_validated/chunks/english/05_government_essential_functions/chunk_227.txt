[Topic: GOVERNMENT ESSENTIAL FUNCTIONS]

#### **Continuity Objectives**

- Maintain availability of essential services nationwide, with priority protection of life-safety and critical loads.
- Preserve public safety and basic order while minimizing rumor, hoarding, and coercion.
- Sustain essential economic participation and protect critical productive capacity.
- Retain essential personnel and safeguard institutional knowledge required to operate the state.
