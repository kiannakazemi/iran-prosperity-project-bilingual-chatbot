#### **Essential Functions to Protect**

In the first 180 days, the following functions must remain operational nationwide, with prioritized allocation of resources during shortages:

- Food availability and distribution.
- Critical supply chains.

- Transportation and logistics.
- Utilities.
- Public health services.
- Emergency response, municipal services, and public order.
- Personnel continuity and institutional knowledge.
