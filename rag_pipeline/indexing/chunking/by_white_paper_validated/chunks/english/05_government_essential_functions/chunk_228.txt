### **Governance and Coordination**

The transitional authority will set national priorities, adjudicate cross-sector allocation decisions under constraint, and ensure consistent provincial implementation through standardized reporting and rapid escalation pathways. Public communication will follow a predictable cadence focused on service priorities, reporting channels, and enforcement against diversion and price gouging.
