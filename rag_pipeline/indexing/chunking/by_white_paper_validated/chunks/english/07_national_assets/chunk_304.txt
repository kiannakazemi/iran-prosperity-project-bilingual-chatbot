## **Subsidiaries Abroad and Shell Companies**

● Numerous subsidiaries and shell companies are maintained in Europe (especially Germany), the Middle East (UAE, Türkiye), and Asia for sanctions evasion, procurement of dual-use technology, and investment of surplus capital. These are often presented as legitimate, independent businesses.
