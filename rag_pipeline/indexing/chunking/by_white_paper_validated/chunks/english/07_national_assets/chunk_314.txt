#### ● **Risk of Sabotage**

○ High risk of deliberate sabotage by regime loyalists, especially within the IRGC. This includes the destruction of critical infrastructure (oil pipelines, power plants), the erasure of digital databases, and the burning of archives to cripple the incoming transitional authority and conceal evidence of past crimes.
