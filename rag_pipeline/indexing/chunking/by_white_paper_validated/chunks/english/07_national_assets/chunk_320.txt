# ● **Digital Asset Backup and Forensic Preservation**

- IT teams will immediately begin creating forensic images of all critical servers and databases.
- Network access to the outside world will be severed to prevent remote wiping of data.
- All available digital records will be preserved as evidence for future legal action.
