[Topic: NATIONAL ASSETS]

#### ● **Risk of Illicit Transfer**

○ The highest and most immediate risk. In a crisis, regime insiders will immediately attempt to transfer liquid assets (electronic funds, cryptocurrency) to pre-established offshore accounts. They will also attempt to smuggle out high-value physical assets like gold, cash, and bearer bonds.
