### **Known Cash Reserves and Gold Holdings**

● The Central Bank of Iran's treasury in Tehran is the primary repository for the nation's gold reserves and physical foreign currency.

● Significant undeclared cash and gold holdings are believed to be stored in secure vaults controlled by the bonyads (especially Bonyad Mostazafan) and the IRGC, outside of the official state system.
