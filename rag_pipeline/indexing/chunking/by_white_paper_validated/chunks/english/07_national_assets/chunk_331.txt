# MONITORING AND EVALUATION

This section outlines the metrics and processes to track progress and ensure accountability.

### **Metrics for Success**

#### ● **Financial**

- Total dollar value of domestic bank accounts frozen.
- Total estimated value of recovered domestic assets in Rials.
- Total estimated value of international assets frozen.
- The quantity of physical gold and cash secured from vaults.
