# ● **Whistleblower Program**

Launch a public campaign encouraging insiders to provide information on hidden assets, offering financial rewards and amnesty where appropriate.

### **Phase 3: Long-Term Measures (Month 2 Onwards)**

#### ● **International Legal Action:**

Initiate legal proceedings in foreign jurisdictions to recover frozen assets. This will involve hiring international law firms and working closely with the justice departments of partner nations.
