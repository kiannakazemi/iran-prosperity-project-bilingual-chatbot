# **Power Plants, Telecom Networks, Transport Hubs**

- Major power generation facilities, the national electricity grid, and the Telecommunication Company of Iran (TCI) are controlled by state-owned or IRGC-linked entities. TCI, for instance, is majority-owned by a consortium linked to Setad and the IRGC.
- Transport hubs like Imam Khomeini International Airport and the ports of Bandar Abbas and Chabahar are under state control, with the IRGC having a pervasive presence in their security and operations.
