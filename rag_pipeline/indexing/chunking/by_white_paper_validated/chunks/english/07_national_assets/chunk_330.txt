# EXPECTED OUTCOMES

These are the clearly defined results that stem from the successful execution of the plan's actions.

- The successful prevention of large-scale asset flight and the physical and digital securing of the majority of the former regime's domestic economic infrastructure within the first 72 hours.
- The legal freezing of a significant portion (estimated in the tens of billions of dollars) of the regime's offshore assets within the first month.
- The preservation of critical evidence needed to support future domestic and international legal proceedings against corrupt officials.
- Increased public trust and confidence in the transitional authority, demonstrated by its decisive action to reclaim national wealth and hold the former regime accountable.
