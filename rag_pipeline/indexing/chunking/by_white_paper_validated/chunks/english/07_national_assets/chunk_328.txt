# **● Leverage Diaspora Expertise and International Cooperation**

- **Diaspora Expertise**: Actively recruit and vet experts from the Iranian diaspora with experience in international finance, forensic accounting, and law. Create a formal advisory council to assist the task force.
- **International Cooperation:** Formalize partnerships with international bodies like the World Bank/UNODC Stolen Asset Recovery (StAR) Initiative, FATF, and Interpol. These organizations can provide technical assistance, training, and a global platform for coordinating legal and diplomatic efforts.
