## RECOMMENDATIONS

These are advisory suggestions that provide strategic direction for the operation.

### **● Establish a Unified Command Structure**

○ Create a single, unified Asset Recovery Task Force, reporting directly to the head of the transitional authority. This task force must be empowered with executive, legal, and operational authority to act decisively and cut through any bureaucratic red tape.
