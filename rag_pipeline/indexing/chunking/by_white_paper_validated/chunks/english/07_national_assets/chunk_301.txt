# **Oil, Gas, and Petrochemical Infrastructure**

- **Facilities:** All major oil fields, refineries (e.g., Abadan, Isfahan), petrochemical plants (e.g., Bandar Imam), and export terminals (e.g., Kharg Island) are under state control via the National Iranian Oil Company (NIOC).
- **Pipelines and Storage:** The national pipeline network and all strategic storage facilities are critical state assets. The IRGC, through Khatam al-Anbiya, has significant influence and control over the construction and security of this infrastructure.
