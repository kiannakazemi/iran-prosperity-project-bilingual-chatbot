### **Cryptocurrencies or Other Digital Assets**

- The regime has utilized cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin (BTC) and Monero (XMR) for sanctions evasion and illicit financing.
- Mining operations have been established within Iran, and assets are held in a combination of domestic and international exchanges, as well as private, un-hosted wallets that are difficult to trace.
