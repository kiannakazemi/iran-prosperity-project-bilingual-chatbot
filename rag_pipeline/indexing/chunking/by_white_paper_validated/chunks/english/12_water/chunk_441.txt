# WATER: Resource Management and Supply Infrastructure

*February 2026*

*Iran is facing a severe stage of water bankruptcy. This plan outlines a set of brief immediate measures to manage and stabilize the country's water sector amid regime collapse. The objective is to protect essential water services, prevent water-related territorial instability during the 180-day emergency phase of the transition period, and establish a sustainable development framework for future water resources management. It addresses urgent threats, promotes equitable water distribution, and recommends essential reforms under the Transitional Water Governance Sector (TWGS).*
