### **Temporary Use of Existing Structures to Prevent a Security Vacuum**

● Until new military and security institutions are fully organized and functional, the Transitional Government must rely on existing structures to maintain public order, defend national sovereignty,

- and secure the country's borders. Defectors who have joined through the National Cooperation Platform, along with revolutionary cells, will play a crucial role in this interim phase.
- Building an entirely new military from the ground is unnecessary, costly, and dangerous.
