### **Modernization of Military Equipment and Technologies**

- The Transitional Government must invest in emerging technologies such as drones, cyber defense, electronic warfare, and artificial intelligence.
- The armed forces must collaborate with universities and research institutions to develop advanced military systems and replace outdated equipment.
