## **Developing Defense Diplomacy and International Cooperation**

- The Transitional Government shall end Iran's militaristic regional adventurism and instead commit to international peacekeeping efforts and United Nations missions.
- Iran must cultivate defense partnerships with regional and global states to strengthen deterrence and promote constructive military engagement.
