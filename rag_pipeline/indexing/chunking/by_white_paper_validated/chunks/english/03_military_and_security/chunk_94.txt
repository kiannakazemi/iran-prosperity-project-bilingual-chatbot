# **Establishing Fair Vetting Committees for Personnel Review**

- The Transitional Government must establish independent institutions to review, on a case-by-case basis, the backgrounds of military and security personnel, ensuring transparency and the right to defense.
- These committees must base their decisions on individual responsibility, not mere organizational affiliation.
- Those who successfully pass this process may be reintegrated into the new institutional structure at the discretion of the Transitional Government.
