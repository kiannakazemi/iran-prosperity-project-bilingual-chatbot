# INSTITUTIONAL MODERNIZATION AND CAPACITY BUILDING

### **Focus on Modern Training and Human Capital Development**

- The Transitional Government must overhaul the military education system to prioritize technical skills, drone and cyber warfare, intelligence analysis, crisis management, and democratic and human rights principles.
- Equal opportunity must be guaranteed for all segments of society.
