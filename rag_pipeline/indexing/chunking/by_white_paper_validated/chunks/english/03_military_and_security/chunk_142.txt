# **Phase A/B2: Days 11 to 40 | Vetting, Disarmament, Neutralization**

# **Strategic Focus**

- Begin the dismantling of regime structures.
- Establish judicial and intelligence control.
- Counterintelligence.

### **Key Actions**

- **Three-Tier Vetting Protocol (Category A/B/C):** Personnel classified for retention, retraining, or prosecution.
- **Transitional Intelligence Tribunal Command (TITC):** Establishing a coordinated mechanism for handling personnel implicated in criminal conduct, including cooperation in the detention of human rights violators and perpetrators of major financial crimes.
- **Controlled Amnesty Framework:** Disarm the regime's militant groups, provide de-radicalization programs, and monitor.
- **Basij and Herasat Dismantlement Program:** Close down university-based and mosque-linked surveillance networks.
- **Counterintelligence Surge Teams (CST):** Sweep ministries, telecom firms, and military units.
- **Aerospace and Naval Transfer:** Retain drone stockpiles, IRGC aircraft, mines, and radar.
- **Joint Airspace Monitoring Protocol**: Coordinate early warning systems with allies.
