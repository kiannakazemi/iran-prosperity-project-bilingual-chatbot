### **Strategic Outcomes by Day 180**

#### ● **Security Agencies**

- The National Intelligence and Security Service (NISS) and the Iranian National Police are fully operational.
- IRGC, Basij, Quds Force, and all other similar institutions and affiliated forces, dissolved.

#### ● **Military Forces**

- Artesh is functioning under civilian command.
- All borders secure; aerospace and navy integrated.
