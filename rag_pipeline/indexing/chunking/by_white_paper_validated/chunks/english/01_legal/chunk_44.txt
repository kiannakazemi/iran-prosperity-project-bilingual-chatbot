- **26.** Legally, Britain and France represent two prominent traditions: common law and civil law, respectively; and politically, they embody two different but equally democratic systems: a parliamentary monarchy and a semi-presidential republic. Unlike France, Britain did not abandon its monarchical tradition but rather gradually evolved into a parliamentary monarchy. This continuity has allowed British lawmakers, over centuries, to master the art of implementing significant legal
