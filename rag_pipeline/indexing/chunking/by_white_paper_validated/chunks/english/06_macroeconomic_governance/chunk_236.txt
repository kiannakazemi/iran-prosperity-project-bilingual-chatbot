### **Importance of Fiscal Policy Stabilization**

Stabilizing fiscal policy is crucial during the transition period, as it enables the government to maintain regular payments, fund critical services, and assert sovereignty over national resources. Without clear fiscal control, disruptions to pensions, subsidies, and administrative operations could erode public confidence and exacerbate economic instability. A credible and functional fiscal framework is also a prerequisite for reengaging with international institutions and unlocking frozen assets.
