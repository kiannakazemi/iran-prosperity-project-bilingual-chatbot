# **Monitoring and Evaluation (Recommended by the World Bank)**

- The first external audits of the pension funds currently under the supervision of the MCLSW (SSO, CSPF, AFPF, and RNPF) will be conducted at the earliest opportunity (if possible, by international accounting firms) and repeated on a periodic basis.
- The central bank will become the independent custodian of those funds "to ensure the physical and legal integrity of the assets and to oversee the transactions of the assets manager" (World Bank, 2003).
