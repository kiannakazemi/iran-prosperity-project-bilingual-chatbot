# STABILIZING FINANCIAL MARKETS

This transition plan outlines the strategic approach for stabilizing Iran's financial markets during the transitional period, focusing on preventing systemic disruption, restoring transparency, protecting national wealth, and rebuilding investor confidence. The plan aims to implement actionable steps—such as targeted market closures, foreign exchange reform, and phased reopenings—while addressing immediate risks of capital flight, asset mispricing, and institutional breakdown.
