# **Key Priorities**

The following measures should be taken promptly:

- Taking control of assets, safeguarding, and recordkeeping.
- Removing political appointees from SOEs and appointing interim management.

- Maintaining the operations of productive enterprises.
- Hiring accounting/auditing firms to oversee past transactions, monitor ongoing finances and perform full risk assessment.
- Assessing liabilities and preserving enterprise value.
- Defining a prioritization framework for privatization.
