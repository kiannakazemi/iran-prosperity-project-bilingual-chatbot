# **Monitoring and Evaluation**

#### **● Metrics for Success**

- Appointment and public disclosure of new leadership at TSE, Securities and Exchange Organization, and related bodies.
- Operational status of key market infrastructure (trading platforms, data systems) and market segments (TSE, corporate debt, Treasury).

- Completion of external audits and asset valuation reviews.
- Adoption of investor protection and disclosure regulations.
- Daily exchange rate spread between official and floating rates.
- Volume of FX transactions processed under each rate.
- Volume of outbound capital transactions blocked or reviewed.
- Number and value of accounts frozen or flagged for legal action.
- Progress in legal review of regime-linked financial holdings.
- Trading volume and participation after phased reopenings.
- Bond yield curve publication and market responsiveness.
