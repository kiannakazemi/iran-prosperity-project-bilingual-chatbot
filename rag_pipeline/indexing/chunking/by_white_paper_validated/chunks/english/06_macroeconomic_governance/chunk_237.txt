### **Objectives**

- Establish economic and administrative stability.
- Assert state sovereignty.
- Preserve basic functions during Iran's transitional phase.

# **Key Priorities**

- Control fiscal and monetary institutions.
- Securing essential public spending.
- Streamline the administrative state as much as feasible.
- Reestablish basic international economic relations.
