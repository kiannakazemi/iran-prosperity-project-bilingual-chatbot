# STABILIZING THE BANKING SYSTEM AND THE CURRENCY

### **Overview**

This transition plan outlines the strategic approach for stabilizing banks and money during the transitional period, focusing on reestablishing monetary authority, stabilizing bank operations, restoring public and foreign confidence, safeguarding financial integrity, and deploying emergency liquidity as needed. The plan aims to implement actionable steps that facilitate a smooth transition while addressing immediate challenges.
