## PRIVATIZATION

# **Overview**

While immediate privatization of assets owned by the government and semi-government organizations may not be feasible in the emergency phase following the fall of the Islamic regime, it is critical that the Transitional Government establish a clear and effective framework to stabilize these assets and prepare them for eventual reform.
