# **Anticipated Political Challenges**

#### **● Challenges**

- Resistance from former regime loyalists.
- Bureaucratic inertia and institutional fragmentation.

- Lack of political consensus on reform priorities.
- Public district and political volatility.
- Interference in technocratic institutions.
