# **Objectives**

The focus during this interim period must be on:

- Securing public property.
- Restoring transparency.
- Ensuring operational continuity.

These steps are essential not only for rebuilding trust in public institutions but also for laying the groundwork for long-term economic recovery and sustainable privatization.
