## **Recommendation**

Act swiftly, prioritizing:

- Securing fiscal control.
- Seeking international recognition.
- Initiating modest but credible administrative reforms.
- Monitor financial stability and public sentiment closely to adjust policies as needed.

# **Expected Outcomes**

- Prevent economic collapse, stabilize inflation, and restore public confidence.
- Unlock critical funds and trade opportunities by reestablishing international engagement.

● Reduce costs and lay the groundwork for long-term governance reforms through administrative streamlining.
