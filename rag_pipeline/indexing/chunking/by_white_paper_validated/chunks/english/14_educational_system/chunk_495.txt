# EDUCATIONAL SYSTEM: Ensuring Continuity and De-Ideologization

*February 2026*

*Reforming Iran's education system requires a phased, multi-year strategy aligned with the evolving institutional, legal, and social conditions of the post-Islamic Republic transition. This strategic document outlines the first stage of reform: Phase 1 (Months 0–6), which focuses on emergency de-ideologization and institutional stabilization.*
