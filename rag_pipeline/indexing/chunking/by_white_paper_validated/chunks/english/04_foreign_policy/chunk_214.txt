### **Handling Other Embassies and Diplomatic Personnel**

For all other ambassadors and mission staff:

- Those without ties to the Islamic Republic's intelligence, security, or ideological apparatus may, with the head of the Transitional Government's approval, continue temporarily in their roles.
- If a senior diplomat is suspected of ideological or security ties, their authority will be immediately transferred to a bureaucratic officer at the mission, pending further orders.
- In all cases, mission performance will be monitored by the central office in Tehran.
