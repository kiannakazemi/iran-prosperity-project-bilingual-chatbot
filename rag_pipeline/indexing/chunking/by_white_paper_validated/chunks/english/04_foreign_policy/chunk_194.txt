### **Three-Month Roadmap**

- **India**: Resume the Chabahar Port project and position it as a commercial hub for southeastern Iran, while also reviving long-term crude oil exports to India's growing market.
- **Japan**: Engage Japan to participate in health, green energy, and reconstruction projects, and restore Iran's role as a stable oil and LNG supplier to Japanese refineries.
- **South Korea**: Resolve the issue of frozen Iranian assets; normalize banking and trade channels; and reestablish crude oil and condensate exports as a foundation for broader cooperation in technology and maritime security.
