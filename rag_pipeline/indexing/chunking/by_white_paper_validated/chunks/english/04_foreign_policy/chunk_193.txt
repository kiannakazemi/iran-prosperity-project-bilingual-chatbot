#### ● **Months 2 to 3**

- Draft a new framework for economic and defense cooperation.
- Define clear limits and mutual expectations—particularly non-interference in domestic and political affairs.
- Sign a "Statement of Principles for Independent and Reciprocal Relations."

## **India, Japan, and South Korea**

As advanced democratic Asian powers, India, Japan, and South Korea will be indispensable partners for Iran in energy, technology, sustainable development, and maritime security.
