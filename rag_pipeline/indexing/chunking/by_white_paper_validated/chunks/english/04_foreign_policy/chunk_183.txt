[Topic: FOREIGN POLICY]

#### ● **Month 1**

- Submit a formal request to remove key sanctions (banking, energy, etc.).
- Issue a public statement of commitment to full cooperation with the IAEA and nuclear transparency.
- Propose bilateral talks and appoint a dedicated negotiation team.

#### ● **Months 2 to 3**

- Begin technical discussions on a phased sanctions removal mechanism.
- Draft a bilateral agreement on trade and investment.
- Initiate dialogue toward a strategic partnership modeled after the U.S.–Iran cooperation in the 1970s.
