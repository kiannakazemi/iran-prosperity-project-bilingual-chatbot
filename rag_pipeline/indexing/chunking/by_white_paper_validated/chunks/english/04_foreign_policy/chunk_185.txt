#### **Three-Month Roadmap**

#### ● **Week 1**

- Issue an official statement:
  - Recognizing the State of Israel.
  - Declaring the end of the Islamic Republic's hostile policies.
  - Inviting direct talks on the Cyrus Accord framework.

#### ● **Month 1**

- Establish a communication channel to initiate consultations.
- Begin discussions on:
  - Opening embassies.
  - Joint efforts to neutralize sabotage by remnants of the Islamic Republic.
  - Cooperation in technology, agriculture, water security, cybersecurity, and energy.
  - A mutual pledge to avoid unilateral or uncoordinated actions.
