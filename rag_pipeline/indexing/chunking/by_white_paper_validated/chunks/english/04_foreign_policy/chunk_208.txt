# **Summary: Proposal for a Comprehensive Regional Stabilization Mechanism**

**Proposed Mechanism:** "*Iran Regional Transition and Stability Framework*"

| Component | Description                                                                                                               |
| --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Objective | Prevent foreign intervention, reduce tensions, and stabilize borders.                                                     |
| Members   | Iran and neighboring states (Türkiye, Iraq, Pakistan, Afghanistan, GCC, etc.).                                            |
| Support   | Monitored by the UN, the EU, and regional institutions.                                                                   |
| Mechanism | Monthly meetings, Border Security Coordination Center, Counterterrorism intelligence sharing.                             |
| Message   | Iran seeks peace, stability, and an active role in regional cooperation—grounded in mutual respect and national interest. |
