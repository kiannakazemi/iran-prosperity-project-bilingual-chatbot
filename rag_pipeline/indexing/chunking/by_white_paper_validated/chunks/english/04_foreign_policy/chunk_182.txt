#### **Three-Month Roadmap**

#### ● **Week 1**

- Send formal communications to the White House, U.S. Department of State, and U.S. Mission to the United Nations, including:
  - Official declaration of the end of the Islamic Republic.
  - Willingness for immediate talks on sanctions and regional stability.
  - Commitment to non-proliferation measures and treaties.
  - Invitation to reopen embassies.
