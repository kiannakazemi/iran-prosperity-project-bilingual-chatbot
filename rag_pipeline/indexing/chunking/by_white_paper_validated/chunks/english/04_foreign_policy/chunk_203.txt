# **The Levant and Iraq**

### **Strategic Objective**

To end Iran's interventionist policies in the Levant and Iraq and replace them with a framework based on trade, peace diplomacy, and joint management of migration, terrorism, and drug trafficking crises.

#### **Actions and Timeline**

#### **● Week 1**

Send formal messages to the governments of Syria, Lebanon, Jordan, and Iraq, declaring:

- The complete termination of support for proxy groups.
- A firm commitment to respect national sovereignty.
- A readiness to cooperate on trade and border security.
