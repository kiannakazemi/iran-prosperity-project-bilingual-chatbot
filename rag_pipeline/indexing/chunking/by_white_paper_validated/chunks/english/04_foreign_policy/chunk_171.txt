### ● **Pakistan**

**Core Objective:** Cooperate on countering cross-border terrorism and separatism. **Initial Agreement Proposal:** Termination of any support for armed groups and joint border monitoring and outposts.

# ● **Afghanistan**

**Core Objective:** Secure the eastern border, manage migration, and combat drug trafficking. **Initial Agreement Proposal:** Develop a joint framework for migration management and require a bilateral commitment to non-interference in internal affairs.
