[Topic: FOREIGN POLICY]

### ● **Month 1**

Bilateral discussions aimed at:

- Activating key transport corridors (such as the INSTC and Sarakhs–Bandar Abbas railway).
- Expanding exports of electricity, gas, and engineering services.
- Launching trilateral consultations between Iran, Turkmenistan, and Azerbaijan.

### ● **Months 2 to 3**

Iran will express its intent to take a more active role in regional economic bodies, such as:

- The Economic Cooperation Organization (ECO).
- Participation in regional rail and road connectivity projects.
