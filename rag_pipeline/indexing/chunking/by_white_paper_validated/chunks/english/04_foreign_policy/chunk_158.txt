### ● **Formal Recognition and Restoration of Full Diplomatic Relations with Israel**

The Transitional Government will officially recognize the State of Israel and initiate normalization efforts without preconditions, marking a historic shift away from ideological hostility.

#### ● **Balanced Relations with China and Russia**

Iran acknowledges the global significance of China and Russia and will strengthen its partnerships with them based on mutual respect, transparency, and Iran's sovereign interests.
