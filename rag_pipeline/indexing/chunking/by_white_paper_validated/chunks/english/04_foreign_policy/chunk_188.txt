# **Three-Month Roadmap**

#### ● **Week 1**

- Send formal communications to the Council of the European Union, the European Commission, and the European Parliament:
  - Announcing the end of the Islamic Republic regime.
  - Proposing enhanced cooperation on shared security challenges.
  - Confirming the release of all wrongfully detained European citizens.
