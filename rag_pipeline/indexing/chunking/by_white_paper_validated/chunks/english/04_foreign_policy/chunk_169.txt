### ● **Iraq**

**Core Objective:** Expand bilateral trade and prevent militias that were previously backed by the IRGC, as well as separatist groups, from crossing into Iran and intervening in its internal affairs. **Initial Agreement Proposal:** Joint border monitoring and terminating the Islamic Republic's proxy

and separatist-group influence.
