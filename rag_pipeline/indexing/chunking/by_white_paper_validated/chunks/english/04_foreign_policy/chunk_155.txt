# CORE PRINCIPLES OF FUTURE IRANIAN FOREIGN POLICY

#### **● Full Transparency in the Nuclear Field**

One of the government's first actions will be to resolve the Iranian nuclear crisis through peaceful and diplomatic means. This means a cessation of any enrichment and reprocessing activities, as well as facilitating unfettered access for the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA) to nuclear sites to work toward the removal of all international sanctions.
