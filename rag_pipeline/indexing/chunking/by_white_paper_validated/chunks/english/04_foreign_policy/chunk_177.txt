### **Immediate Actions in Cooperation with Neighbors**

### ● **Official Declarations from Neighboring States**

The Transitional Government will reaffirm its commitment to the principle of good neighborliness and extend a hand of friendship to all neighboring countries, emphasizing mutual respect, peaceful coexistence, non-interference, and cooperation.
