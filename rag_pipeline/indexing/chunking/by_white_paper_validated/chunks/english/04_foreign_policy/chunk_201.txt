#### **Actions and Timeline**

#### **● Week 1**

- The Transitional Government will send formal diplomatic letters to all GCC member states, reaffirming:
  - Commitment to good neighborliness and non-intervention.

- Respect for the territorial integrity and political sovereignty of Arab states.
- An invitation to launch regional security and economic dialogue.
