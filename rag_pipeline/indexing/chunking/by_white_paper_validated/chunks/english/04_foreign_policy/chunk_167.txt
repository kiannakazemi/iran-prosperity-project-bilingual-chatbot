### IRAN AND NEIGHBORING COUNTRIES

# **Week One: Official Messages to Neighboring Countries**

### ● **Objective**

Prevent misunderstandings, reaffirm commitment to good neighborly relations, and invite regional cooperation.

#### ● **Actions**

The Transitional Government will issue formal diplomatic communications to all neighboring states, outlining the following reciprocal commitments:

- A reaffirmation of friendship and a pledge of mutual non-interference.
- A commitment to secure borders on both sides to prevent the infiltration of armed and terrorist groups.
- A mutual guarantee of respect for each other's territorial integrity.

- An open invitation to launch a regional dialogue aimed at stabilization and cooperation.
- A pledge to uphold existing bilateral agreements on trade, transit, and customs, to combat drug trafficking, and to avoid any disruption of these commitments, while agreeing to negotiate certain arrangements at an appropriate time.
