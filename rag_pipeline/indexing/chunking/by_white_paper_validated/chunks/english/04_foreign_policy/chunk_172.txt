#### ● **Saudi Arabia**

**Core Objective:** Initiate de-escalation and redefine regional ties.

**Initial Agreement Proposal:** Joint statement condemning proxy warfare and fostering regional détente.

#### ● **Qatar**

**Core Objective:** Establishing a mutual commitment to non-interference in internal affairs, ending support for affiliates of the Islamic Republic, and expanding bilateral trade.

**Initial Agreement Proposal:** MOU on regional stability cooperation and joint non-intervention declaration.
