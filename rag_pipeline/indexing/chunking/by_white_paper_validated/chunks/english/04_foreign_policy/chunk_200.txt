# **The Persian Gulf and Arabian Peninsula**

# **Strategic Objective**

Iran will seek to rebuild relations with the member states of the Persian Gulf Cooperation Council (GCC), end sectarian and ideological rivalries, and establish a regional framework for cooperation on security, energy, and maritime transit.
