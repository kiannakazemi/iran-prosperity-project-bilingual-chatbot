# EMERGENCY FOREIGN MINISTRY RECONSTRUCTION PLAN DURING TRANSITION

In the early days of the transition, the Ministry of Foreign Affairs must come under the full political, administrative, and strategic control of the Transitional Government. The objective is to restore international connectivity, prevent diplomatic vacuums, and initiate institutional rebuilding grounded in national interests and professional diplomacy.
