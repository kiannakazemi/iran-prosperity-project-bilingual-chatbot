### **Key Missions and Action Items**

### **Short-Term (First 3 to 6 Months)**

- Draft a comprehensive roadmap to strengthen economic diplomacy and attract foreign direct investment (FDI) through a coordinated interagency effort.
- Produce an Iran Economic Opportunities Booklet in the six UN official languages (namely English, French, Spanish, Arabic, Chinese, and Russian) as well as in German and, if need be, other languages.
- Launch a multilingual online investment platform for Iran.
- Participate in at least three major global economic conferences (e.g., Davos, OECD, JETRO, GITEX).
- Host investment opportunity workshops in strategic countries.
