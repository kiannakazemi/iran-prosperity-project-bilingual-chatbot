### **Actions and Timeline**

#### **● Week 1**

Send formal diplomatic outreach to Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Azerbaijan, Georgia, and Armenia, including:

- An expression of readiness to deepen economic and transit ties.
- A request for joint efforts to secure borders and counter smuggling and extremism.
