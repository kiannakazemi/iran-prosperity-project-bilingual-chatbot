# **International Institutions**

Iran will immediately and comprehensively resume its engagement with international organizations. At the forefront of this effort is cooperation with the IAEA.

# **International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA)**

- **● Week 1**
  - Submit a formal letter to the IAEA:
    - Declaring readiness for unrestricted inspections of all nuclear sites.
    - Committing to full transparency and cooperation.
