### ● **Turkmenistan**

**Core Objective:** Preserve stability along the northeast border and ongoing energy partnerships. **Initial Agreement Proposal:** Border and energy agreements and continuation of joint gas and transit projects.

# **Key Topics for Bilateral Negotiation**

### ● **Security and Borders**

- Immediate cooperation on border management.
- Preventing arms and militant infiltration into each other's territory.
- Establishment of Joint Border Operations Rooms with each neighboring country.
