#### **Other International Institutions**

#### **● Week 1**

○ Formally notify the International Monetary Fund (IMF) and the World Bank of the establishment of the Transitional Government and its intention to maintain and deepen cooperation as the new sovereign authority.

#### ● **Month 1**

- Begin discussions with both institutions for technical and financial assistance to support stabilization, public finance reform, and critical service restoration.
- Establish joint working groups on governance, public health, and education.
