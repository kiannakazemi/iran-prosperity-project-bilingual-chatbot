# IRAN'S ROLE IN REGIONAL ORDER AND MULTILATERAL COOPERATION FRAMEWORKS

During the transitional period, Iran must redefine and solidify its role as an anchor of regional stability. Guided by a non-ideological foreign policy, the Transitional Government will seek to end the country's isolation and actively participate in shaping a new regional security and economic architecture.

Iran's regional policy will center on three strategic geographic zones:

- The Persian Gulf and Arabian Peninsula.
- The Levant and Iraq.
- Central Asia and the South Caucasus.
