# **Proposed Task Force Structure**

| Section                          | Responsibilities                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Central Secretariat              | Strategic planning, coordination with the government economic team, and supervision of embassy-level operations.    |
| Public Sector Investment Office  | Engage with governments, development funds, and multilateral banks (IMF, World Bank, ADB).                          |
| Corporate Engagement Unit        | Interface with multinationals, SMEs, and global entrepreneurs.                                                      |
| Public Economic Diplomacy Office | Craft Iran's investment messaging, media campaigns, and international content.                                      |
| Events and Outreach Division     | Organize conferences, expos, investment summits, and "Iran Economic Week."                                          |
| International Opportunity Office | Active presence in global economic forums, workshops in target countries, and promotion of Iranian projects abroad. |
