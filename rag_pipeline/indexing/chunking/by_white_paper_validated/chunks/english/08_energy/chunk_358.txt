### ● **Review Process**

- Daily Situation Reports compiled by TECU regional command centers and aggregated at HQ.
- Weekly National Energy Briefings shared with the Crown Prince's Transitional Authority and international liaisons.
- Monthly Independent Oversight Summaries to be released publicly, covering restoration, safety, and staffing KPIs.
