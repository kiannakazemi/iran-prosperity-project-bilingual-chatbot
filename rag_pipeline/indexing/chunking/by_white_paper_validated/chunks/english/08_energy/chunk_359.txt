# ANTICIPATED POLITICAL CHALLENGES

### ● **Resistance from entrenched regime-linked personnel at the provincial level**

Many mid- and upper-level energy officials have personal or financial ties to the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC). Removing them may trigger local sabotage or bureaucratic delays.

**Mitigation:** Immediate dismissal of top two ranks with simultaneous deployment of TECU-trained replacements backed by visible enforcement units.
