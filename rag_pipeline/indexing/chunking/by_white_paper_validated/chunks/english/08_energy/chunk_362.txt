### ● **Opposition to transparency and audit-driven accountability**

Regime beneficiaries and corrupt actors may obstruct or discredit TECU's transparency efforts (e.g., public dashboards, contract reviews).

**Mitigation:** Tie transparency to national dignity and recovery. Empower public watchdog groups and international observers to amplify legitimacy.
