### ● **Delayed international financial support**

Uncertainty over legal recognition or geopolitical risk may slow the release of frozen assets or emergency credit.

**Mitigation:** Coordinate early with international partners. Secure pre-drafted credit instruments and public commitments during pre-entry (Phase 0).
