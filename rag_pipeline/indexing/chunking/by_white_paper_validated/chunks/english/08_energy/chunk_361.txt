#### ● **Sabotage and espionage during early SCADA or facility handovers**

IRGC remnants may exploit digital or physical access points to sabotage key infrastructure during the transition window.

**Mitigation:** Isolate all SCADA systems on Day 1, purge foreign-linked VPNs, and deploy plainclothes counter-sabotage units with rapid intervention authority.
