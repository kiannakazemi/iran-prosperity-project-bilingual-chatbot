## **Key Priorities**

- Use military and postal fleets for a central "push" distribution model until private wholesalers revive.
- Ensure power and water restoration to flagship referral hospitals as a confidence signal.
- Deploy mobile IPC units with chlorine generators and Personal Protective Equipment to high‑density communities, camps, and shelters.
