# **Emergency Phase Transition Plan (Day 0 to 180) — Overall Objective**

Sequencing relief, stabilization, and institutionalization across a 180‑day horizon protects life while signaling a credible pathway toward a sustainable national health service. Such staging mirrors the "emergency‑to‑early recovery‑to‑development" framework recommended by WHO and OCHA for complex crises [1].
