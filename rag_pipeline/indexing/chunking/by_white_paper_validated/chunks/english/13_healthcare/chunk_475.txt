# **Expected Outcomes**

By Hour 72, the OC publishes its first situation report; the DMCP holds a national contact roster for trauma facilities; and EMS dispatch lines are live in every provincial capital.

# **Monitoring and Evaluation**

Daily situation reports track ICU beds, blood products, essential medications, and EMS response times, while weekly red‑team drills stress‑test communications. All data feeds an open‑access dashboard to foster public trust.
