### **Assumptions**

- Core road corridors remain open or can be protected under security arrangements.
- WHO, ICRC, and Red Crescent partners will provide immediate in‑kind trauma and IPC (infection prevention and control) support.
- Major public hospitals are structurally intact but need utility restoration.
