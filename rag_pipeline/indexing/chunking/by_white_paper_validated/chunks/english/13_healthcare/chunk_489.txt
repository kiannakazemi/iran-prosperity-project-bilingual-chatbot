#### **Actions and Recommendations**

Refurbish or erect modular UCCs with point‑of‑care diagnostics; deploy tele‑health pods in sparsely populated areas; build EHOS on open‑source DHIS2 (District Health Information Software 2) / OpenMRS (Open Medical Record System); and publish anonymized datasets to crowd‑source outbreak detection.
