line with the Iranian people's wish to replace foreign words with Persian equivalents, this white paper uses the term "*Mehestan*" instead of the Arabic term for the legislative branch, "Majles-e Shora."

the same reason mentioned in the previous footnote, the term "*Divan*" is used instead of the Arabic term for the judiciary, "Qoveh Qaza'ieh."

this white paper, "absolute majority" refers to more than half of *all* members agreeing on an issue, while "simple majority" means the majority of the members *present* at a meeting agree on that issue.
