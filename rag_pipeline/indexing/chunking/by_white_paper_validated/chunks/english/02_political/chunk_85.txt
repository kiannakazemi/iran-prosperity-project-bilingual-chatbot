- **19.7.4.** Nature and Duration of the Transitional Justice Program:
  - **19.7.4.1** The Program is temporary in nature and shall dissolve once all referred cases have been decided.
  - **19.7.4.2** The Program's maximum duration shall be five years, with an optional one-year extension approved by the head of the Divan.
