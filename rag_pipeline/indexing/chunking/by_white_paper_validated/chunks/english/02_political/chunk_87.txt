# III. TIMELINE

- **20.** The political process described in this white paper is expected to take between 18 and 24 months. In the event of unforeseen circumstances, such as natural disasters or war, the timeline may be extended. However, in such cases:
  - **20.1.** Any extension exceeding six months must be approved jointly by the heads of all three transitional institutions (the Transitional Mehestan, the Transitional Government, and the Transitional Divan) as well as the Leader of the National Uprising; and
  - **20.2.** Any extension beyond 12 months shall also require a national referendum for the nation's approval.
