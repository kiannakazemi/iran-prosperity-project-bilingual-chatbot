# **Challenge 3: Smuggling, looting, or illicit markets during transition**

- **Risk**: As legal systems reset, illicit trade in fuel, steel, and medicines may rise.
- **Mitigation**: Customs task force under ISTF with border coordination; fast-track industrial zoning, police presence; tech-enabled tracking.
