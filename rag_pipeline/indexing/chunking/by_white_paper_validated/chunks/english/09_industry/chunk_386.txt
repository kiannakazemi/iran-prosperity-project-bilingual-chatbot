### **Challenge 6: Bureaucratic inertia from former regime-affiliated managers**

- **Risk**: Some mid-level administrators may slow implementation or sabotage reforms.
- **Mitigation**: Use vetting and loyalty review; pair transitional leadership with local counterparts on interim contracts; rotate regional leadership as needed.
