# ASSUMPTIONS

- Territorial and energy security are largely stabilized (per TECU Phase 1–2 completion).
- Domestic fuel and electricity flows are resuming in 70%+ of provinces.
- The transitional authority has initial public legitimacy and international recognition.
- IRGC and Bonyad-linked industrial assets have been partially frozen or secured.
- Short-term import credit lines or barter arrangements are available with neighboring countries and Western allies.
- Time-bound capital controls and transparent timelines will be maintained while monetary/exchange frameworks shift to a floating or crawling-peg regime, and FATF engagement begins.
