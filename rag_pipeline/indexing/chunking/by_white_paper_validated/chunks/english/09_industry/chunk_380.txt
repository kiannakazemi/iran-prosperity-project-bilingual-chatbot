#### **● Public Engagement and Communication:**

○ Number of town halls, stakeholder roundtables, and local-diaspora coordination sessions.

#### **● Review Process:**

- Biweekly reporting to the Crown Prince's Transitional Council.
- Public performance dashboards are updated at 30, 90, and 150 days to build public trust.
- External technical audits at Days 60 and 180, conducted by international observers.
- Feedback from industrial unions, integrated quarterly into revised policies.
