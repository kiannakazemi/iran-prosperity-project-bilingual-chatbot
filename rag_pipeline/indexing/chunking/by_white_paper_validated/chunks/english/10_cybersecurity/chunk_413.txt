### ● **Backlash against tightening incident reporting and external audit powers**

Operators may frame mandatory reporting and audits as "politicization," cost burdens, or national-security exposure, delaying compliance.

**Mitigation:** Mandating time-bound incident reporting and regulated supervision as national resilience requirements, with clear thresholds and protected reporting channels.
