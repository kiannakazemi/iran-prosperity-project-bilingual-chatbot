# **Phase 3: Defensive Stabilization and Hardening (Days 41 to 100)**

**Objectives:** Introducing modern secure platforms alongside legacy systems.

- Deploying secure edge gateways and data aggregation nodes to decouple monitoring from legacy SCADA environments.
- Migrating non-critical monitoring and reporting functions to secure cloud-integrated platforms.
- Launching pilot projects for segmented substation automation and secure engineering access workflows.
- Establishing standardized zero-trust access models for operators, engineers, and vendors.
- **●** Expanding monitoring coverage to substations, plants, and high-value field locations.

**Outcomes:** Modern monitoring and supervisory capabilities begin operating in parallel with legacy control systems.
