### ● **Disinformation framing cyber reform as "foreign control"**

Hardliners may portray adoption of international norms and cooperation as surrendering sovereignty.

**Mitigation:** Positioning reforms as sovereignty-strengthening (resilience, continuity of essential services), publishing transparent performance metrics, and emphasizing domestic ownership of standards and enforcement.
