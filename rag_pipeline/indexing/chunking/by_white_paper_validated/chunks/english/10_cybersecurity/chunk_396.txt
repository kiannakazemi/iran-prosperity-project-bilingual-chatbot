**Change Control Enforcement:** Critical configuration changes to industrial control logic, routing systems, and security devices require dual authorization and are recorded in centralized logs.

**Segregation of Duties:** Operational authority, system administration, and cybersecurity oversight functions are separated to prevent unilateral, unmonitored control over high-impact systems.

**Legal and Administrative Safeguards:** Emergency authorities are established to allow temporary suspension of access privileges, reassignment of duties, and immediate credential revocation when suspicious activity is detected.
