### ● **Delay or conditionality in international technical support and procurement**

Geopolitical uncertainty can slow access to telecom equipment, security tooling, and capacity-building support.

**Mitigation:** Pre-negotiating technical assistance frameworks, diversifying suppliers, and standardizing secure procurement and lifecycle obligations.
