### ● **Resistance to executive-level liability and board-level cyber accountability**

Senior leaders may oppose governance models that make management bodies explicitly responsible for cyber risk measures.

**Mitigation:** Codifying leadership accountability and oversight duties as part of national cyber governance, tied to audits and enforcement.
