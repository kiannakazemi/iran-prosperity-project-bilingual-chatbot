## OBJECTIVES

- Stabilize and protect the country's most critical ecosystems to prevent further irreversible environmental loss.
- Mitigate immediate pollution threats to safeguard public health.
- Rebuild institutional trust and international cooperation through transparency, civic engagement, and partnerships with global environmental bodies.
- Establish the foundation for long-term environmental reform, transparency, and sustainable management frameworks.
