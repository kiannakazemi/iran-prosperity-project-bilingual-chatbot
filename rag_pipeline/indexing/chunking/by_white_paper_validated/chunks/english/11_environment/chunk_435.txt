# EXPECTED OUTCOMES

- Emergency environmental governance is activated and operational.
- Critical ecosystems are placed under legal protection, with initial stabilization underway.
- Immediate pollution threats are addressed in select high-risk polluted areas.
- Continuity of environmental institutions is maintained, with reform planning initiated.
- Re-engagement with international institutions and donors is underway.
- Public legitimacy and trust are strengthened through visible action and inclusion.
- Strategic frameworks for long-term environmental transition have been initiated.
