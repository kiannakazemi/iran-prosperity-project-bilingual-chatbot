### **Review Process:**

- The Transitional Environmental Emergency Task Force (TEETF) will provide monthly internal progress reports and quarterly public briefings to maintain transparency.
- An independent evaluation mechanism, composed of national experts and international observers selected by stakeholders, will validate results and ensure credibility in reporting.
- Progress data and outcomes will be made publicly accessible via open reporting platforms in line with global standards on environmental transparency and accountability.
